# 🎨 The Aesthetics (CSS)

> *"Style is a way to say who you are without having to speak."*

This folder contains the visual soul of the **Endless Hugot** sanctuary. It handles the colors, the moods, and the "glassmorphism" effects that make the experience feel soft and safe.

---

## ✨ Key Styles
* **`gradientShift`**: A 20-second infinite animation that moves the background like a slow sunset.
* **`heartbeat`**: A scale-based animation applied to the main heart to make it feel "alive."
* **`glassmorphism`**: Uses `backdrop-filter: blur()` to give the cards that frosted glass look.
* **`clamp()` Dynamics**: Ensuring the text and cards look perfect on a tiny mobile screen or a large desktop.

---

## 🛠️ Maintenance
If you want to change the "vibe" of the sanctuary:
1. Update the `--background` linear-gradient values.
2. Adjust the `transition` speeds to make the hover effects faster or slower.

*Keep it soft. Keep it with a touch of pink.* 🌸
